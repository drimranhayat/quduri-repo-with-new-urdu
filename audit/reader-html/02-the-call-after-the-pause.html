02

The call after the pause

Receiving revelation
Sahih
Arabic · Published text

عَنْ أبي سَلَمَةَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ أَنَّ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الأَنْصَارِيِّ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: وَهُوَ يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ فَتْرَةِ الوَحْيِ، فَقَالَ فِي حَدِيثِهِ: «بَيْنَا أَنَا أَمْشِي إِذْ سَمِعْتُ صَوْتًا مِنَ السَّمَاءِ، فَرَفَعْتُ بَصَرِي، فَإِذَا المَلَكُ الَّذِي جَاءَنِي بِحِرَاءٍ جَالِسٌ عَلَى كُرْسِيٍّ بَيْنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالأَرْضِ، فَرُعِبْتُ مِنْهُ، فَرَجَعْتُ فَقُلْتُ: زَمِّلُونِي، فَأَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ تَعَالَى: {يَا أَيُّهَا المُدَّثِّرُ1 قُمْ فَأَنْذِرْ} [المدثر: 2] إِلَى قَوْلِهِ {وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ} [المدثر: 5]. فَحَمِيَ الوَحْيُ وَتَتَابَعَ».

English · Translation

Abu Salamah ibn ‘Abdur-Rahmān reported that Jābir ibn ‘Abdullah al-Ansāri (may Allah be pleased with him and his father) said: While narrating about the halt in revelation, he said in his Hadīth: "While I was walking, I heard a voice from the sky. I looked up, and there was the angel who had come to me at Hirā’, sitting on a chair between the heaven and the earth. I was terrified of him, so I returned and said: Cover me! Then Allah Almighty revealed: {O you covered [in your cloak], arise and warn} [Surat al-Muddaththir: 1-2] until His saying: {and shun idol worshiping.} [Surat al-Muddaththir: 5] After that, the revelation became intense and continuous."

Urdu · Translation

ابو سلمہ بن عبد الرحمن سے روایت ہے کہ جابر بن عبد اللہ انصاری رضی اللہ عنہما نے وحی کے توقف کے زمانے کا ذکر کرتے ہوئے اپنی حدیث میں فرمایا: "میں چل رہا تھا کہ اچانک میں نے آسمان سے ایک آواز سنی، میں نے اپنی نظر اٹھائی تو کیا دیکھتا ہوں کہ وہی فرشتہ جو میرے پاس حرا میں آیا تھا آسمان اور زمین کے درمیان ایک کرسی پر بیٹھا ہوا تھا۔ میں اس سے بہت ڈر گیا، چنانچہ میں واپس آیا اور کہا: مجھے کپڑا اوڑھا دو، تو اللہ تعالیٰ نے یہ آیات نازل فرمائیں: {يَا أَيُّهَا المُدَّثِّرُ1 قُمْ فَأَنْذِرْ} سے لے کر {وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ} تک۔ پھر کثرت سے اور مسلسل وحی آنے لگی۔

Sahih al-Bukhari 4 ↗Source attribution: Agreed upon
English explanation+
The Prophet (ﷺ), while recounting the halt in revelation at the beginning of his mission, said: As I was walking through the alleys of Makkah, I suddenly heard a voice from the sky. I looked up, and there was the angel Jibrīl (Gabriel)—the same one who had come to me in the cave of Hirā’—sitting on a chair between the sky and the earth. I was filled with fear and dread of him, so I returned to my family and said: Wrap me in a cloak. So Allah Almighty revealed: {O you covered} in your cloak, {arise} to call people to the message, {and warn} those who have not yet believed in your message. {And your Lord}—your only true God—{proclaim His greatness} and praise and exalt Him. {And your garments} and clothes {purify them} and clean them from impurities. {And shun the filth}, i.e., the worship of idols and false gods, {and keep away from it} completely. After that, the revelation became strong and continuous.

Lessons and benefits+
  • The revelation to the Prophet (ﷺ) ceased for a short period after the descent of Allah's word: {Read}.
  • It is permissible for a person to speak about the hardships he experienced after they have passed, as an expression of gratitude to Allah Almighty.
  • The first revelation after: {Read in the name of your Lord Who created} was Allah's saying: {O you covered [in your cloak]}.
  • The grace of Allah Almighty upon His Prophet (ﷺ) is shown in that He continuously bestowed revelation upon him without interruption until the Prophet (ﷺ) departed from this world.
  • The obligation to call to Allah Almighty, warning those who turn away, and giving glad tidings to those who obey.
  • The obligation to purify one's clothes for prayer, as inferred from Allah's saying: {And purify your garments}.
  • The obligation to believe in the angels and in what Allah has empowered them to do of tasks and other matters.

Meaning and understanding

01Was al-Muddaththir the beginning of all revelation, or the renewal after its pause?
This account concerns revelation after a pause. The publisher places the initial command to read before that pause and the opening of al-Muddaththir after it. The angel was the same one who had previously come at Hira. Keeping that sequence clear prevents confusing the two occasions.
Check the supporting passage
The revelation to the Prophet (ﷺ) ceased for a short period after the descent of Allah's word: {Read}.

02What responsibilities accompany the command to arise and warn?
The explanation joins calling people to the message with proclaiming Allah's greatness, purifying garments, and abandoning idol worship. The passage moves from being covered after fear to carrying the responsibility of proclamation. It describes duties, not merely an emotional experience.
Check the supporting passage
So Allah Almighty revealed: {O you covered} in your cloak, {arise} to call people to the message, {and warn} those who have not yet believed in your message.

03What does the account show about fear and receiving the command to proclaim the message?
The Prophet describes being terrified and asking to be covered. The command to arise and warn follows that experience, and revelation then becomes continuous. The account does not present the initial fear as refusal to carry the message.
Check the supporting passage
I was terrified of him, so I returned and said: Cover me!

04Which practical aspect of purification does the publisher identify?
The explanation speaks of cleaning garments from impurities, and the sixth benefit connects the verse with purifying clothes for prayer. This is a practical instruction alongside proclaiming Allah's greatness and rejecting idol worship.
