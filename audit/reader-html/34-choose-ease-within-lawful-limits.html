34

Choose ease within lawful limits

A balanced Prophetic path
Sahih
Arabic · Published text

عَنْ عَائِشَةَ أُمِّ المُؤْمِنينَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهَا أَنَّهَا قَالَتْ: مَا خُيِّرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ بَيْنَ أَمْرَيْنِ إِلَّا أَخَذَ أَيْسَرَهُمَا، مَا لَمْ يَكُنْ إِثْمًا، فَإِنْ كَانَ إِثْمًا كَانَ أَبْعَدَ النَّاسِ مِنْهُ، وَمَا انْتَقَمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لِنَفْسِهِ إِلَّا أَنْ تُنْتَهَكَ حُرْمَةُ اللَّهِ، فَيَنْتَقِمَ لِلَّهِ بِهَا.

English · Translation

‘Ā’ishah, Mother of the Believers, (may Allah be pleased with her) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) was never given a choice between two things except that he would choose the easier of the two unless it were a sin. If it were a sin, he would be the farthest of people from it. The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) never took revenge for himself, but when the prohibitions of Allah were violated, he would take revenge for the sake of Allah.

Urdu · Translation

امّ المؤمنین عائشہ رضی اللہ عنہا سے روایت ہے، وہ کہتی ہيں: جب بھی رسول اللہ ﷺ کو دو چیزوں میں سے ایک کا انتخاب کرنا ہوتا، تو آپ ﷺ ہمیشہ دونوں میں سے آسان کا انتخاب کرتے، بشرط کہ وہ آسان کام گناہ نہ ہوتا۔ اگر وہ گناہ ہوتا، تو آپ ﷺ اس سے سب سے زیادہ دور رہتے۔ رسول اللہ ﷺ نے کبھی اپنی ذات کے لیے انتقام نہیں لیا، ما سوا اس کے کہ اللہ کی حرمات میں سے کسی حرمت کو پامال کیا جائے۔ اس صورت میں آپ ﷺ اللہ تعالی کے لیے انتقام لیا کرتے تھے۔

Sahih al-Bukhari 3560 ↗Source attribution: Agreed upon
English explanation+
‘Ā’ishah, Mother of the Believers (may Allah be pleased with her), informed about some of the morals of the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him). Among what she mentioned was that whenever the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) was given a choice between two matters, he would choose the easier of the two, as long as the easier option did not lead to sin. If it led to sin, he would be the farthest of people from it and would then choose the more difficult option. The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) never sought revenge for himself exclusively; rather, he would forgive and overlook his own rights. But when the sanctities of Allah were violated, he would seek revenge for the sake of Allah, and he was the most intense in anger for the sake of Allah.

Lessons and benefits+
  • It is recommended to choose the easier option in matters, as long as it does not involve disobedience to Allah.
  • The ease of Islam.
  • The legitimacy of getting angry for the sake of Allah Almighty.
  • The Prophet's forbearance, patience, and adherence to the truth in enforcing the limits set by Allah Almighty.
  • Ibn Hajar said: It indicates refraining from choosing the difficult option, being content with ease, and abstaining from insistence on matters that are not necessary.
  • Encouraging forgiveness except when it comes to the rights of Allah Almighty.

Meaning and understanding

01What makes the choice of ease principled rather than a search for convenience at any cost?
Aishah states the limit expressly: the easier option must not be sinful. If it involved sin, the Prophet kept far from it. Ease is therefore a preference within obedience, not a rule that makes any convenient choice acceptable.
Check the supporting passage

02How does the account distinguish personal forbearance from concern for Allah's limits?
He did not seek revenge merely for himself, yet the report distinguishes violations of Allah's sanctities. The explanation describes forgiveness of personal injury alongside adherence to divine limits. The distinction does not turn every private grievance or angry reaction into enforcement of Allah's rights.
