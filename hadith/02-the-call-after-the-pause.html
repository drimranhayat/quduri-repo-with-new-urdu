The call after the pause

الدَّعْوَةُ بَعْدَ فَتْرَةِ الْوَحْيِ

وقفۂ وحی کے بعد دعوت کا حکم

Read the hadith

Arabic source text

Text from the cited collection, including its chain where present. Page divisions and wording follow the linked digital edition.

٤ - قَالَ ابْنُ شِهَابٍ : وَأَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ : أَنَّ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللهِ الْأَنْصَارِيَّ قَالَ، وَهُوَ يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ فَتْرَةِ الْوَحْيِ، فَقَالَ فِي حَدِيثِهِ: «بَيْنَا أَنَا أَمْشِي إِذْ سَمِعْتُ صَوْتًا مِنَ السَّمَاءِ فَرَفَعْتُ بَصَرِي، فَإِذَا الْمَلَكُ الَّذِي جَاءَنِي بِحِرَاءٍ جَالِسٌ عَلَى كُرْسِيٍّ بَيْنَ السَّمَاءِ وَالْأَرْضِ، فَرُعِبْتُ مِنْهُ، فَرَجَعْتُ فَقُلْتُ: زَمِّلُونِي، فَأَنْزَلَ اللهُ تَعَالَى: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ * قُمْ فَأَنْذِرْ﴾ إِلَى ⦗٨⦘ قَوْلِهِ ﴿وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ﴾ فَحَمِيَ الْوَحْيُ وَتَتَابَعَ».
صحيح البخاري (١/ ٧)

URDU · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

ابو سلمہ بن عبد الرحمن سے روایت ہے کہ جابر بن عبد اللہ انصاری رضی اللہ عنہما نے وحی کے توقف کے زمانے کا ذکر کرتے ہوئے اپنی حدیث میں فرمایا: "میں چل رہا تھا کہ اچانک میں نے آسمان سے ایک آواز سنی، میں نے اپنی نظر اٹھائی تو کیا دیکھتا ہوں کہ وہی فرشتہ جو میرے پاس حرا میں آیا تھا آسمان اور زمین کے درمیان ایک کرسی پر بیٹھا ہوا تھا۔ میں اس سے بہت ڈر گیا، چنانچہ میں واپس آیا اور کہا: مجھے کپڑا اوڑھا دو، تو اللہ تعالیٰ نے یہ آیات نازل فرمائیں: {يَا أَيُّهَا المُدَّثِّرُ1 قُمْ فَأَنْذِرْ} سے لے کر {وَالرُّجْزَ فَاهْجُرْ} تک۔ پھر کثرت سے اور مسلسل وحی آنے لگی۔

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

Abu Salamah ibn ‘Abdur-Rahmān reported that Jābir ibn ‘Abdullah al-Ansāri (may Allah be pleased with him and his father) said: While narrating about the halt in revelation, he said in his Hadīth: "While I was walking, I heard a voice from the sky. I looked up, and there was the angel who had come to me at Hirā’, sitting on a chair between the heaven and the earth. I was terrified of him, so I returned and said: Cover me! Then Allah Almighty revealed: {O you covered [in your cloak], arise and warn} [Surat al-Muddaththir: 1-2] until His saying: {and shun idol worshiping.} [Surat al-Muddaththir: 5] After that, the revelation became intense and continuous."

English summary

These verses of al-Muddaththir followed the pause in revelation; the passage distinguishes this from the first revelation.

Meaning and understanding

01Was al-Muddaththir the beginning of all revelation, or the renewal after its pause?
This account concerns revelation after a pause. The publisher places the initial command to read before that pause and the opening of al-Muddaththir after it. The angel was the same one who had previously come at Hira. Keeping that sequence clear prevents confusing the two occasions.
02What responsibilities accompany the command to arise and warn?
The explanation joins calling people to the message with proclaiming Allah's greatness, purifying garments, and abandoning idol worship. The passage moves from being covered after fear to carrying the responsibility of proclamation. It describes duties, not merely an emotional experience.
03What does the account show about fear and receiving the command to proclaim the message?
The Prophet describes being terrified and asking to be covered. The command to arise and warn follows that experience, and revelation then becomes continuous. The account does not present the initial fear as refusal to carry the message.
04Which practical aspect of purification does the publisher identify?
The explanation speaks of cleaning garments from impurities, and the sixth benefit connects the verse with purifying clothes for prayer. This is a practical instruction alongside proclaiming Allah's greatness and rejecting idol worship.
