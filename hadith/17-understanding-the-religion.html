Understanding the religion

التَّفَقُّهُ فِي الدِّينِ

دین کی سمجھ حاصل کرنا

Read the hadith

Arabic source text

٧١ - حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ عُفَيْرٍ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا ابْنُ وَهْبٍ، عَنْ يُونُسَ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ قَالَ: قَالَ حُمَيْدُ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ: سَمِعْتُ مُعَاوِيَةَ خَطِيبًا يَقُولُ: سَمِعْتُ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ يَقُولُ: «مَنْ يُرِدِ اللهُ بِهِ خَيْرًا يُفَقِّهْهُ فِي الدِّينِ، وَإِنَّمَا أَنَا قَاسِمٌ وَاللهُ يُعْطِي، وَلَنْ تَزَالَ هَذِهِ الْأُمَّةُ قَائِمَةً عَلَى أَمْرِ اللهِ لَا يَضُرُّهُمْ مَنْ خَالَفَهُمْ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ أَمْرُ اللهِ».
صحيح البخاري (١/ ٢٥)

URDU · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

معاویہ رضی اللہ عنہ سے روایت ہے، وہ کہتے ہیں کہ میں نے اللہ کے رسول صلی اللہ علیہ و سلم کو کہتے ہوئے سنا ہے : ”جس کے ساتھ اللہ خیر کا ارادہ کرتا ہے، اسے دین کی سمجھ عطا کر دیتا ہے۔ میں تو بس تقسیم کرنے والا ہوں، دینے والا اللہ ہے۔ یہ امت اللہ کے دین پو قائم رہے گی، اس کی مخالفت کرنے والے اسے نقصان نہیں پہنچا سکیں گے، یہاں تک کہ اللہ کا امر آجائے“۔

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

Mu‘āwiyah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I heard the Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) say: "When Allah intends good for someone, He grants him the understanding of the religion. Indeed, I am only a distributor, and Allah is the Giver. This Ummah will continue to follow Allah's laws, not being harmed by those who oppose them, until Allah's command comes to pass."

Meaning and understanding

01What is meant by understanding the religion as a sign of good?
The report identifies understanding the religion as a gift from Allah. The benefits consequently urge seeking and learning religious knowledge. Its focus is understanding the guidance, rather than equating religious good with collecting information, public recognition, or success in argument.
02Why does the Prophet distinguish distributing from giving?
He describes himself as a distributor and Allah as the Giver. The explanation includes sustenance and knowledge among what Allah granted him to distribute. The distinction recognizes the means through which a benefit arrives while affirming its dependence on Allah.
03What continuity does the closing assurance concern?
It concerns continued adherence to Allah's laws within the Ummah despite opposition. The benefits explain that if one group forsakes the truth, another will follow it. This is not a statement that every member will always agree or that believers will experience no worldly difficulty.
