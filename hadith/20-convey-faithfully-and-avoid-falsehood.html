Convey faithfully and avoid falsehood

التَّبْلِيغُ بِأَمَانَةٍ وَاجْتِنَابُ الْكَذِبِ

امانت کے ساتھ نقل اور جھوٹ سے اجتناب

٣٤٦١ - حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَاصِمٍ الضَّحَّاكُ بْنُ مَخْلَدٍ أَخْبَرَنَا الْأَوْزَاعِيُّ حَدَّثَنَا حَسَّانُ بْنُ عَطِيَّةَ عَنْ أَبِي كَبْشَةَ عَنْ عَبْدِ اللهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ﷺ قَالَ: «بَلِّغُوا عَنِّي وَلَوْ آيَةً وَحَدِّثُوا عَنْ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ وَلَا حَرَجَ وَمَنْ كَذَبَ عَلَيَّ مُتَعَمِّدًا فَلْيَتَبَوَّأْ مَقْعَدَهُ مِنَ النَّارِ.»
صحيح البخاري (٤/ ١٧٠)

URDU · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

عبداللہ بن عمرو رضی اللہ عنہما سے روایت ہے کہ اللہ کے نبی ﷺ نے فرمایا: ”میری طرف سے لوگوں کو پہنچا دو، اگرچہ ایک آیت ہی ہو۔ بنی اسرائیل سے روایت کرو، ان سے بیان کرنے میں کوئی حرج نہیں ہے۔ جس نے مجھ پر جان بوجھ کر جھوٹ باندھا، وہ اپنا ٹھکانہ جہنم میں بنا لے“۔

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Amr (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Convey from me even if one verse, and narrate from the Children of Israel, and there is no sin in doing that. And whoever intentionally tells a lie about me, let him occupy his seat in Hellfire."

English summary

Al-Khattabi distinguishes the command to convey from permission to report Israelite accounts and stresses reliable transmission from the Prophet.

Meaning and understanding

01How does conveying even a little remain compatible with responsible teaching?
The explanation permits passing on a small amount, provided the person knows what they convey. The closing warning against deliberate false attribution makes accuracy essential. Limited knowledge is not a reason to invent missing material, but neither must a learner know everything before responsibly sharing something verified.
02What limit applies to narrating accounts of the Children of Israel?
The explanation permits such narration when it does not conflict with the Shariah. Permission to mention an account is not a declaration that every earlier story is authenticated as Prophetic teaching. The distinction matters when selecting historical or illustrative material for a lesson.
