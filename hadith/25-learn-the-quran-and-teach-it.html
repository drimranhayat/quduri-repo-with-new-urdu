Learn the Quran and teach it

تَعَلُّمُ الْقُرْآنِ وَتَعْلِيمُهُ

قرآن سیکھنا اور سکھانا

Read the hadith

Arabic source text

Text from the cited collection, including its chain where present. Page divisions and wording follow the linked digital edition.

٥٠٢٧ - حَدَّثَنَا حَجَّاجُ بْنُ مِنْهَالٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ قَالَ: أَخْبَرَنِي عَلْقَمَةُ بْنُ مَرْثَدٍ: سَمِعْتُ سَعْدَ بْنَ عُبَيْدَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ السُّلَمِيِّ، عَنْ عُثْمَانَ ﵁، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ قَالَ: «خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ»، قَالَ: وَأَقْرَأَ أَبُو عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ فِي إِمْرَةِ عُثْمَانَ حَتَّى كَانَ الْحَجَّاجُ، قَالَ: وَذَاكَ الَّذِي أَقْعَدَنِي مَقْعَدِي هَذَا.
صحيح البخاري (٦/ ١٩٢)

URDU · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

عثمان رضی اللہ عنہ سے روایت ہے کہ اللہ کے نبی صلی اللہ علیہ و سلم نے فرمایا : ”تم میں سب سے بہتر شخص وہ ہے جو قرآن سیکھے اور اسے سکھائے“۔

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

‘Uthmān ibn ‘Affān (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Prophet (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The best of you are those who learn the Qur’an and teach it."

The commentator explains the excellence of learning and teaching the Quran without treating it as an unrestricted ranking in every respect.

Meaning and understanding

01What does learning and teaching the Quran include beyond pronunciation?
The explanation includes recitation, memorization, measured reading, understanding, and interpretation, together with acting on the knowledge. The benefits also mention meanings and rulings. Teaching thus involves responsibly conveying what one has learned, rather than treating fluent reading as the whole of Quranic education.
02Why are learning and teaching praised together?
Learning equips a person with knowledge; teaching enables others to benefit from it. The source praises those who do not keep their learning entirely to themselves. The explanation also links both activities with practice, so conveying knowledge does not replace responding to it personally.
