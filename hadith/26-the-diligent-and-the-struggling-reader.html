The diligent and the struggling reader

الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ وَمَنْ يَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ

ماہر قاری اور مشقت سے پڑھنے والا

Read the hadith

Arabic source text

Text from the cited collection, including its chain where present. Page divisions and wording follow the linked digital edition.

٢٤٤ - (٧٩٨) حَدَّثَنَا قُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ، وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ عُبَيْدٍ الْغُبَرِيُّ ، جَمِيعًا عَنْ أَبِي عَوَانَةَ . قَالَ ابْنُ عُبَيْدٍ: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو عَوَانَةَ ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ ، عَنْ زُرَارَةَ بْنِ أَوْفَى ، عَنْ سَعْدِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ قَالَتْ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: « الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ، وَالَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَيَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ وَهُوَ عَلَيْهِ شَاقٌّ لَهُ أَجْرَانِ ».
صحيح مسلم (٢/ ١٩٥)

URDU · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

اُم المومنین عائشہ رضی اللہ عنہا سے روایت ہے کہ رسول اللہ ﷺ نے فرمایا: ”جو شخص قرآن کے پڑھنے میں ماہر ہو، وہ معزز اور نیک سفراء (فرشتوں) کے ساتھ ہو گا، اور جو شخص قرآن کو اٹک اٹک کر پڑھتا ہو اور اسے پڑھنے میں مشقت ہوتی ہو تو اس کو دو گنا ثواب ملتا ہے“۔

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

‘Ā’ishah (may Allah be pleased with her) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "The proficient reciter of the Qur’an will be with the noble and obedient angel-scribes, and the one who recites the Qur’an stutteringly and finds it difficult will have two rewards."

English summary

Al-Nawawi explains both rewards and states that the two rewards do not make the struggling reader superior to the proficient one.

Meaning and understanding

01How does the report encourage both proficiency and perseverance?
It gives the proficient reciter the distinction of being with the noble and obedient angel-scribes, while promising two rewards to the reader who continues despite difficulty. The explanation identifies rewards for recitation and struggle. Both descriptions encourage learning; neither makes avoidable mistakes a goal.
02Do two rewards mean that struggling is necessarily superior to proficiency?
The quoted benefit expressly rejects that conclusion. The proficient reciter has a special rank and reward; the number two should not be used to erase it. A struggling learner is encouraged to continue while improving, rather than deliberately remaining inaccurate.
