Learning together and helping others

مُدَارَسَةُ الْعِلْمِ وَإِعَانَةُ الْآخَرِينَ

مل کر علم سیکھنا اور دوسروں کی مدد کرنا

Arabic source text

Text from the cited collection, including its chain where present. Page divisions and wording follow the linked digital edition.

٣٨ - (٢٦٩٩) حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ يَحْيَى التَّمِيمِيُّ وَأَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ وَمُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْعَلَاءِ الْهَمْدَانِيُّ (وَاللَّفْظُ لِيَحْيَى) قَالَ يَحْيَى: أَخْبَرَنَا، وَقَالَ الْآخَرَانِ: حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مُعَاوِيَةَ ، عَنِ الْأَعْمَشِ ، عَنْ أَبِي صَالِحٍ ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ: قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ ﷺ: « مَنْ نَفَّسَ عَنْ مُؤْمِنٍ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ الدُّنْيَا نَفَّسَ اللهُ عَنْهُ كُرْبَةً مِنْ كُرَبِ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَمَنْ يَسَّرَ عَلَى مُعْسِرٍ يَسَّرَ اللهُ عَلَيْهِ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، وَمَنْ سَتَرَ مُسْلِمًا سَتَرَهُ اللهُ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، وَاللهُ فِي عَوْنِ الْعَبْدِ مَا كَانَ الْعَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ، وَمَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ، وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللهِ يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللهِ، وَيَتَدَارَسُونَهُ بَيْنَهُمْ إِلَّا نَزَلَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِينَةُ، وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ، وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ، وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللهُ فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ، وَمَنْ بَطَّأَ بِهِ عَمَلُهُ لَمْ يُسْرِعْ بِهِ نَسَبُهُ .»

ابو ہریرہ رضی اللہ عنہ سے روایت ہے، وہ کہتے ہیں کہ اللہ کے رسول ﷺ نے فرمایا: ”جس نے کسی مومن کی دنیا کی پریشانیوں میں سے کوئی پریشانی دور کردی، اللہ تعالیٰ بروز قیامت اس کی پریشانیوں میں سے ایک پریشانی دور کردے گا۔ جس نے کسی تنگ دست پر آسانی کی، اللہ اس پر دنیا وآخرت میں آسانی کرے گا۔ جس نے کسی مسلمان کی عیب پوشی کی، اللہ تعالیٰ دنیا و آخرت میں اس کی عیب پوشی فرمائے گا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ بندے کی مدد کرتا رہتا ہے جب تک بندہ اپنے بھائی کی مدد کرتا رہتا ہے۔ جو شخص علم کی تلاش میں کسی راستے پر چلا اللہ تعالیٰ اس كی وجہ سے اس کے لیے جنت کی راہ آسان کردیتا ہے۔ جب کوئی قوم اللہ کے کسی گھر میں جمع ہو کر اللہ کی کتاب کی تلاوت کرتی ہے اور اسے آپس میں پڑھتی پڑھاتی ہے، تو ان پر سکینت کا نزول ہوتا ہے، (اللہ تعالیٰ کی) رحمت ان کو ڈھانپ لیتی ہے اور اللہ اپنے پاس موجود فرشتوں میں ان کا ذکر کرتا ہے۔ اور جس کا عمل اسے پیچھے کردے، اس کا نسب اسے آگے نہیں لے جاسکتا۔“

ENGLISH · TRANSLATION OF THE MEANING

Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Whoever relieves a believer from one of the hardships of this world, Allah will relieve him from one of the hardships of the Day of Judgment. Whoever makes things easy for someone in difficulty, Allah will make things easy for him in this world and the Hereafter. Whoever covers a Muslim, Allah will cover him in this world and the Hereafter. Allah is in the aid of the slave as long as the slave is in the aid of his brother. Whoever treads a path seeking knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise. No group of people gather in one of the houses of Allah, reciting the Book of Allah and studying it among themselves, except that tranquility descends upon them, mercy envelops them, the angels surround them, and Allah mentions them to those near Him. And whoever is slowed down by his deeds will not be hastened forward by his lineage."

Short questions & answers · 7

Editorial learning questions. Read the hadith and the cited commentary alongside these exercises.

Meaning and understanding

01How are relieving hardship and helping others related to recompense?
The report promises corresponding divine relief for easing a believer's difficulty and states that Allah helps a person who helps their brother. The explanation includes practical assistance, money, and supplication. Service is therefore broader than a single form of charity, while the promised relief should not be replaced with an invented fixed worldly payment.
02When can concealing a fault become protection of wrongdoing?
The benefits distinguish a private slip from persistence in corruption and harm. Concealment must not cancel correcting evil; the source discusses referring persistent wrongdoing to those in authority while considering the risk of greater harm. The instruction should not be used as a blanket reason to hide ongoing harm.
03What joins the promise about seeking knowledge with the blessing of Quran study gatherings?
The report encourages a path of learning and describes gathering to recite and study the Quran. The explanation connects sincere pursuit of religious knowledge with a path to Paradise, and names tranquillity, mercy, angels, and Allah's mention among the gathering's blessings. These are reasons for serious study, not substitutes for learning accurately.
04Why does the narration end by mentioning lineage?
It warns that ancestry cannot make up for deficient deeds. The explanation specifically cautions against relying on noble forefathers while neglecting one's own conduct. This closing point brings the preceding encouragements back to personal action: helping, learning, and responding to guidance.
05What distinct promise is given for easing the situation of someone in difficulty?
The report promises ease from Allah in both this world and the Hereafter. This is stated separately from the promise of relief on the Day of Judgment for relieving a believer's worldly hardship.
06Must the merit of joint Quran study be restricted to the particular mosque setting named in the narration?
The narration names one of Allah's houses. The quoted explanation from al-Nawawi also includes similar gatherings in a school or comparable setting, Allah willing. That extension is identified as commentary rather than silently inserted into the transmitted wording.
07What positive promise accompanies concealing another Muslim's fault?
The narration promises that Allah will cover the person in this world and the Hereafter. The explanation concerns faults that should not be exposed; the qualification about persistent harmful conduct must be kept alongside this encouragement.
